Each topic is searched on every enabled source below. Just type keywords — word variants are matched automatically (thermosphere ≈ thermospheric) and papers are kept only if the keywords appear close together in the title or abstract. For precise control use quotes and AND / OR / NOT, e.g. "thermospheric density" AND (SINDy OR "reduced order"). Follow a journal, author or institution: journal:Nature, author:Daniele Sicoli, institution:West Virginia University — add | keywords to filter inside, e.g. journal:Nature | space weather.
OpenAlex and Crossref cover all fields; Semantic Scholar is strongest in CS, physics and engineering; Europe PMC is life sciences and medicine. Duplicates across sources are merged.
OpenAlex gives ~100 free searches/day per connection without a key, ~1,000 with a free account key from openalex.org/settings/api. The key stays on this device only.
